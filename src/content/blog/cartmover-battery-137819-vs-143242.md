If you run Appleton CartMover™ cart tuggers, you may find two different battery part numbers in your fleet: **#137819** and **#143242**. Both are 24V packs, which makes it easy to assume they're interchangeable. They aren't the same battery, so it pays to know which one each unit takes.

## The Two Packs at a Glance

| | [Appleton #137819](/parts/appleton-137819-battery) | [Appleton #143242](/parts/appleton-143242-battery) |
|---|---|---|
| Voltage | 24V | 24V |
| Chemistry (our replacement) | NiCd | NiMH |
| Capacity (our replacement) | 3000mAh | 9000mAh |
| Rated cycle life | 800+ cycles | 1,000+ cycles |
| Operating temperature | -40°C to 65°C | -20°C to 60°C |
| Best for | Cold storage, unheated docks, harsh areas | Long shifts and heavy carts |

## Appleton #137819: The Cold-Weather Workhorse

Our replacement for #137819 is a 24V NiCd pack. NiCd is a proven chemistry that holds up in cold storage, on unheated docks and in other harsh areas, and it handles the stop-and-start work of tugging carts all day.

**Choose it if:** your unit's current pack is labeled 137819.

## Appleton #143242: More Energy per Charge

Our replacement for #143242 is a 24V NiMH pack with 9000mAh of capacity, three times the 3000mAh of the NiCd pack above. More stored energy means more cart pulls between charges, which matters on long shifts and with heavy carts.

**Choose it if:** your unit's current pack is labeled 143242.

## Can You Switch From One to the Other?

Don't assume so. Matching voltage is only one part of compatibility. Different part numbers can mean differences in physical size, connector and charger requirements, and a pack that doesn't fit your unit or charger can't do its job.

The safe rule is simple: **order the part number printed on your current battery's label.** If you want to move a unit from one pack to the other, [contact us first](#contact) so we can confirm it will work with your unit and charger.
