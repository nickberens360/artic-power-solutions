An Appleton RollMover™ or CartMover™ is a hard-working machine, and regular maintenance keeps it that way. Consistent care extends the life of the equipment and its battery, keeps operators safe and catches small issues before they turn into downtime. It's also a cornerstone of [operating roll movers safely](/blog/safe-workplace).

Use this checklist alongside your owner's manual, which always takes priority for your specific model.

## Daily Checks (Before Each Shift)

- **Look for visible damage.** Check the frame, handle and wheels for cracks, dents or loose parts.
- **Check the battery level.** Make sure the pack has enough charge for the shift ahead.
- **Test the controls.** Confirm the throttle works in both directions and responds smoothly.
- **Test the safety controls.** Make sure the emergency stop or reverse control and any horn or alarm work as they should.
- **Clear debris.** Remove shrink wrap, strapping or paper scraps from around the wheels.

## Weekly Checks

- **Clean the wheels and rollers.** Remove anything wrapped around them or embedded in the surface.
- **Inspect the battery connector and terminals.** Look for corrosion, looseness or damaged wiring, and make sure the pack is fully seated.
- **Wipe down the unit.** Keep dust and debris from building up, especially around the motor.
- **Lubricate where specified.** Follow your owner's manual for lubrication points and intervals.

## Monthly Checks

- **Inspect all wiring.** Look for frayed, pinched or damaged wires and make sure connections are secure.
- **Check the charger and cables.** Make sure the charger works correctly and the cables and plugs are in good condition.
- **Inspect the drive components.** Look for wear on the wheels and listen for new noises during operation.
- **Note battery runtime.** Compare how long a full charge lasts against previous months. A steady drop is the earliest sign a pack is wearing out.

## Battery Care Habits

The battery is the part most likely to take a mover out of service, and how you charge and store it affects how long it lasts.

- **Use the right charger.** Charge only with a charger that matches the pack's voltage and chemistry, and follow its instructions.
- **Charge in a suitable spot.** Use a designated, well-ventilated area away from heat sources.
- **Avoid extreme heat.** Heat shortens battery life. Don't store packs near heaters, in direct sun or in hot vehicles.
- **Store packs charged.** If a unit will sit unused for a while, store the battery charged and top it up periodically, following the manufacturer's guidance.
- **Plan replacement.** When runtime no longer covers a shift, order a replacement before the pack fails completely.

When it's time for a new pack, match the Appleton part number on the label:

## Annual Professional Inspection

In-house checks catch most issues, but it's worth having each unit inspected by Appleton Mfg. or a qualified material-handling technician at least once a year. They can check the motor, drive and electrical system in more depth than a daily walk-around allows.
